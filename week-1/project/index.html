How this works and its limits

Onset rule (ICPAC). The rains have started on the first day of a 5-day spell with at least 20 mm of rain and at least 3 rain days (1 mm or more), as long as no dry spell longer than 7 days follows in the next 21 days.

Data. Past seasons come from the ERA5 reanalysis through the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API. This season and the 16-day forecast come from the Open-Meteo Forecast API.

Limits. The data is gridded (cells of roughly 9 to 25 km), not a physical gauge on your farm. The fixed seasons (Mar to May, Oct to Dec) fit areas with two rainy seasons, such as most of central and eastern Kenya. They do not fit western Kenya and parts of the Rift Valley, which have one long season. This is a decision aid, not agronomic advice.
